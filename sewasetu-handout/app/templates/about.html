Scheme and service information

Eligibility and timing

The applicant must be 60 years or above on the application date. New applications close at {{ deadline_label }}. Pending applications reaching the 15-day decision limit are processed by the statutory deemed-approval job.

What to prepare

Enter the name as on age proof and DOB as DD/MM/YYYY. Use a readable JPG, JPEG or unencrypted PDF up to 5MiB. The inherited portal lists birth certificate, school-leaving certificate or voter ID as age-proof examples.

Keep bank account digits, including leading zeros, and the 11-character IFSC. This portal checks format and does not verify actual bank ownership.

One application per person

Use an existing pending record for corrections instead of applying again. Start a separate application only for a different eligible person.

Shared phones and existing records

Distinct adults can use one contact number. A verified contact can view associated records; a phone is not a universal person identifier. Original records and application numbers are retained. Some inherited records have no document file; tracking still works without reapplying.

Correct a pending application

Verify the contact, open the record and choose Correct application. Review changed values and use a fresh code for sensitive changes. ID, number, contact, decision and original submission date cannot be edited. A racing decision or newer edit stops a stale correction.

After a decision, self-service correction is unavailable. A verified assisted correction channel has not been supplied. Do not delete and reapply to hide a mistake.

Assessment SMS simulation

The required SMS console and inbox are public. Delivered codes can be read there. This simulation cannot provide real-world SMS confidentiality.
