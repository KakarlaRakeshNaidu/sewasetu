Before you start

  1. Have access to a mobile number for a one-time verification code.
  2. Use the applicant's full name and date of birth as on age proof.
  3. Keep the bank account number and IFSC as text, including leading zeros.
  4. Upload a readable JPG/JPEG or PDF up to 5MiB (5,242,880 bytes).
  5. Review all details and confirm the declaration.

Adults may share one contact number. Anyone who controls that phone can access applications linked to it; this does not provide separate private identities.
